# ADR-001 — New Zealand First

## Decision

TIAKI launches with New Zealand as the initial market.

## Initial Domain

Halal slaughter and meat processing.

## Rationale

The first product must solve a narrow, measurable operational problem before expanding internationally.

## Expansion

Future markets can be introduced through configurable:

- Jurisdictions
- Halal requirements
- Certification bodies
- Accreditation bodies
- Recognition arrangements
- Export market requirements
- OMAR / market rules
