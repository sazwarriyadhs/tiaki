# TIAKI

## Meat Industry Intelligence

**From Farm to Processing. From Traceability to Global Markets.**

TIAKI is an intelligent digital platform built for the meat industry — connecting livestock, slaughter operations, meat processing, quality assurance, inventory, cold-chain operations, traceability, and export workflows in one connected ecosystem.

Initially focused on New Zealand, TIAKI is designed to help meat businesses improve operational visibility, strengthen product traceability, organise evidence, identify risks, and make better-informed decisions across their supply chains.

From abattoirs and meat processors to manufacturers, cold-storage operators, distributors, and exporters, TIAKI aims to connect fragmented operational information into a more transparent, measurable, and evidence-driven environment.

**One platform. Connected operations. Traceability across the meat value chain.**

---

## Our Vision

To help build a more connected, efficient, transparent, and traceable global meat industry — from livestock and raw materials to finished products and international markets.

TIAKI is designed for the broader meat ecosystem. Halal assurance is an important specialised capability, alongside operational intelligence, quality management, food-safety workflows, inventory visibility, and export traceability.

## Built for the Entire Meat Industry

TIAKI is designed to support organisations throughout the meat value chain:

- **Abattoirs and Slaughter Facilities** — Animal intake, batch records, slaughter events, inspection records, and production traceability.
- **Meat Processing Companies** — Carcass identification, cutting operations, production batches, packaging, and product genealogy.
- **Meat Manufacturers** — Raw materials, ingredients, processing records, finished products, and batch relationships.
- **Cold Storage and Warehousing** — Inventory visibility, batch locations, storage records, and temperature-monitoring integrations.
- **Distributors and Wholesalers** — Product identification, stock movements, batch visibility, and supply-chain coordination.
- **Exporters and International Suppliers** — Consignment traceability, supporting documents, and destination-market information.
- **Integrated Meat Enterprises** — Connected workflows, teams, facilities, and operational oversight.

Workflows and integrations depend on each organisation's requirements, available data, and operational environment.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Operations Intelligence

Connect slaughter, production, processing, inventory, and operational information to improve visibility across business activities.

### 2. End-to-End Traceability

Link livestock and incoming materials to slaughter events, carcasses, processing batches, packaged products, and outbound consignments.

### 3. Quality and Assurance Management

Organise inspection records, quality checks, non-conformances, corrective actions, and supporting evidence.

### 4. Food Safety and Compliance Support

Support structured operational records, documentation, review workflows, and relevant control processes.

### 5. Halal Assurance Intelligence

Support halal-related process records, traceability, evidence management, exception handling, and audit preparation for businesses serving halal markets.

### 6. Cold-Chain and Equipment Intelligence

Where suitable integrations are available, connect temperature readings, equipment signals, monitoring data, and operational alerts.

### 7. Supply Chain and Export Intelligence

Connect batch information, inventory movements, consignment records, and export-supporting documentation.

### 8. AI-Assisted Risk Intelligence

Help identify missing information, inconsistent records, unusual patterns, and potential operational exceptions that may need human review.

## AI-Powered. Evidence-Led. Human-Governed.

TIAKI's intelligence capabilities are intended to help users understand operational context and prioritise information that needs attention.

Potential use cases include:

- Missing, inconsistent, or disconnected records
- Unusual production events and potential process deviations
- Batch-level traceability and investigation workflows
- Evidence retrieval and contextual summaries
- Connected equipment and environmental monitoring
- Exception prioritisation and follow-up workflows
- Production and supply-chain visibility
- Export-document preparation support

AI-generated outputs are decision-support signals, not proof of compliance. They may be incomplete or incorrect and must be reviewed by appropriately authorised personnel before consequential decisions are made.

## A Platform for the Industry — Not Just One Organisation

TIAKI is intended to support the wider meat-industry ecosystem, including producers, slaughter facilities, processors, manufacturers, storage operators, distributors, exporters, assurance providers, auditors, technology partners, and relevant regulatory stakeholders.

TIAKI is not limited to a single certification body or customer organisation. Its value comes from helping businesses connect their operations and, where appropriate, exchange traceability information with authorised supply-chain partners.

## Governance and Accountability

TIAKI is designed to support responsible digital operations through structured records, role-based access, audit logging, and human-led review workflows.

TIAKI does not independently grant halal certification, replace required inspections, or assume the authority of regulators, certification bodies, veterinarians, auditors, or other authorised decision-makers.

The platform aims to make information more accessible, connected, and actionable for the people responsible for operating and overseeing meat businesses.
